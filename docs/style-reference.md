# Style reference

Approved pilot: SC00000.m1-m72 (in script/sos/SC0/SC00000.tsv, checked in-game: work/qa/pilot). Match this voice.
Tone: teen ecchi gag comedy with sincere moments. Natural, snappy American English; contractions; no stiff textbook
phrasing. Jokes, perverted gags and tsukkomi keep their punch.

## Conventions
- Nameplate speaker keys stay Japanese in the data (they select a graphic); never translate the speaker column.
- 「...」 inside a line -> "..."; 『title』 -> "title" or 'title'.
- …… / ・・・ -> "..." (a line that is only …… -> "......"). ！？ -> "?!". ～ stretches a word ("Sakurai-kuuun") or goes.
- Inner thoughts: JP （...） -> (...). Narration in full-width-space-indented lines is one paragraph in English.
- Readings in parentheses after names (見月（みつき）そはら) are furigana for the player: drop them.
- Onomatopoeia: English/romanised ("Gwah!", "Kyaa!", "Ugh...", "Heh heh heh..."); never kana.
- Yen amounts stay yen ("10 million yen"). Japanese foods: keep the common name (taiyaki, takoyaki, yakisoba,
  dagashi) with a word of English if needed for the joke.
- Choices (300 px, one line, ~40 chars): short imperative or phrase ("Grovel", "Flip the table").
